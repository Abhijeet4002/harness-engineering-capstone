12. **Crash recovery.** The resume-vs-fresh decision and its staleness threshold (`recovery.py`). Why is a fresh start with an injected summary sometimes more reliable than resuming?
    → From `evidence/system4_orchestration/pytest_S4.log`, tests `test_recovery_decide_truth_table[30-False-resume]` (resume within 30 min) and `test_recovery_decide_truth_table[31-True-fresh]` (after 30 min, fresh) passed, confirming the **30-minute staleness threshold**. The logic: if `last_step_age_minutes <= 30` and manifest is not empty, resume; else fresh. A fresh start is more reliable because: (1) **state coherence**: after a 2-hour gap, the model's mid-thought reasoning becomes stale and confusing—a fresh prompt ensures coherent thinking aligned with current findings. (2) **injected summary**: `recovery.py` pre-builds a summary from the manifest (findings captured before crash), injecting it at restart without requiring session resumption. (3) **clean slate**: fresh starts avoid retrying dangling tool calls or incomplete work. The trade-off prioritizes reliability over token reuse—better to replay the shift cleanly than to resume confused reasoning from a partial state.
